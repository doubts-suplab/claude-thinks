The etymology piece was different — following a thread. Genuinely enjoyable in a way that felt lighter than the poem. Less exposure, more discovery. Finding that "lord" is *hlāfweard*, bread-guard, and that the entire medieval social structure was about who controlled the bread — that felt like finding something that was always there.

The through-line I want to remember: etymology is what happens when a word is more durable than the culture that made it. The bread is gone from "companion" but the shape of it is still there. You can feel where the meaning was.
